    The name

    Means heart.

    Bruce Blair
    CEO, Cardiac Media

    Kevin Knight showed me what was possible beyond what I already knew. He had a nickname from his college days, Cardiac, earned because of a heart condition he carried. He wore it like a badge, and named his company Cardiac Productions. After he was gone, I asked his wife Joanna if I could carry the name forward. She said yes.

    Read the full storyClose

    He introduced me to media, to technology, and to the idea that creativity and technical skill could live in the same person. Kevin passed away in 2009 from H1N1. He left behind a legacy bigger than any production he ever ran.

    I changed Productions to Media because what I do has grown into something broader than any one category, but the name took on something more than a tribute in the process.

    Serving people, helping organizations communicate better, equipping teams to do more than they thought they could. That is not just a job description. It is why I do this. Ministry has a word for that kind of calling, and I have never found a better one.

    The name carries Kevin's memory. It also carries the reason I keep showing up.
